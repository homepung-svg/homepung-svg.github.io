About the Game

TING TING TANG TANG is a mobile arcade game featuring bouncing projectiles, chain reactions, upgrades and high-score challenges.

Fire your shots, create powerful ricochets, and compete to achieve incredible scores!

Game Modes & Leaderboards

CLASSIC - Aim for your highest score in regular gameplay.

10 MIN CHALLENGE - Compete for the highest score within ten minutes.

Google Play Games leaderboards are planned for an upcoming game update.
