1. Introduction

HOMEPUNG ("we", "us", or "our") develops mobile games, including TING TING TANG TANG. We respect user privacy and explain how information may be collected, used, shared and protected.

This policy covers TING TING TANG TANG and its associated online services. Other HOMEPUNG games may have different data practices and policies.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, information may include:

  • Gameplay scores, achievements and statistics.
  • Locally saved game progress and preferences.
  • Google Play Games player identifier and game profile information when connected.
  • Leaderboard scores, ranking and related game activity.
  • Advertising identifiers, IP addresses, app interactions and diagnostic information processed through advertising services.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us for support.

We do not need your Google account password to provide game leaderboards.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and improve game features.
  • Save progress, preferences and high scores.
  • Authenticate players through Google Play Games.
  • Submit scores and display online rankings.
  • Display advertisements and measure their performance.
  • Maintain security, diagnose errors and prevent abuse.
  • Respond to support requests.

4. Advertising - Google AdMob

Our game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information including IP addresses, advertising or device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information.

Such information may be used for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, subject to applicable settings and laws. Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on user choices and applicable requirements.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

5. Google Play Games Services

When Google Play Games features are available and used, Google may process player identifiers, game profile information and leaderboard scores.

Online leaderboards may display your Google Play Games player name, ranking and game score according to Google Play Games privacy settings.

Google manages the Play Games platform and associated account data under its own policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers necessary for game operations, including:

  • Google AdMob for advertising.
  • Google Play Games Services for player authentication and online leaderboards.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting this website.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or to protect the service.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Game settings, progress and high scores may be stored locally on your device. Removing the game or clearing application data may remove locally stored information.

Leaderboard records and advertising-related information may be retained by Google according to its service policies.

Support communications are retained only as reasonably necessary for support, legal obligations and legitimate operational purposes.

8. Security and International Processing

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Third-party services may process information in countries other than your country of residence, subject to applicable privacy safeguards.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may manage advertising preferences through your device and Google account settings.

You may also manage your Play Games profile and related privacy settings through Google.

To ask about information processed directly by HOMEPUNG, including applicable access or deletion requests, contact us below.

Please note that removing local game data does not automatically delete information maintained by independent third-party services.

10. Children's Privacy

We recognize the importance of protecting children's personal information. Where required, age-appropriate safeguards and parental consent must be applied.

Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions or concerns relating to a child.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when game features, service providers or applicable requirements change. The current policy and effective date will be published on this page.
